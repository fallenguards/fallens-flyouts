# Fallen's Flyouts

Make your own flyouts on your action bars, for World of Warcraft: Forever.

Pick up a spell, an item or a macro and drop it on the plus that appears on an action bar button. It is stacked on that button. Press the button's key, or click it, and the flyout opens; press 1, 2 or 3 to cast what is inside. It works in combat and with a controller.

Shift-drag an icon to move it inside its flyout, or drag it out to take it off. Right-click an icon takes it off too. Right-click the button to give the flyout its own icon. `/ff` opens the settings, where the profiles are. The game's Keybindings options have a Fallen's Flyouts section: a key for each flyout and for each icon of an open one.
