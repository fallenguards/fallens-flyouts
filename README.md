# Fallen's Flyouts

Make your own flyouts on your action bars, for World of Warcraft: Forever.

Pick up a spell, an item or a macro and drop it on the plus that appears on an action bar button. It is stacked on that button. Press the button's key, or click it, and the flyout opens; press 1, 2 or 3 to cast what is inside. It works in combat and with a controller.

Right-click an icon in a flyout to take it off, or Shift-drag it out. Right-click the button to give the flyout its own icon. `/ff` opens the settings, where the profiles are.

Report a problem or ask for something under **Issues**.
